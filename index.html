About Us

Serious about cannabis. Not much else.

Welcome to Pipe & Leaf — with not one, but two awesome spots in the heart of Fairbanks, Alaska. Founded by a Fairbanks family, we're the local hub for all things green and dank. Track us down at 899 Old Steese Highway and 3546 Airport Way, like a treasure hunt for the best bud in town.

Our menu is a buffet of good vibes: classic strains with a Ph.D. in relaxation, edibles that taste better than grandma's cookies, and concentrates and vape carts that hit harder than a bear hug. We're the masters of papers, bongs, and pipes too.

Our crew? Not your typical buttoned-up experts — more like your buddies who happen to know a ton about weed. Whether you're a joint-rolling Jedi, a bong enthusiast, or a newbie trying not to cough your lungs out, our budtenders will take you to high places.
